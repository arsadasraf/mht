GUIDE Departmental Data Collection & System Ingestion Protocol
Operational Readiness SOP

To ensure a flawless transition into live production on the Machine House (MH) ERP platform, all department heads must adhere strictly to the following 3-step master data submission protocol:

Step 1: Download Standard Excel Templates

Department leads will receive standardized .xlsx sheets matching the exact column names specified in Section 3 of this document. Columns must not be renamed, deleted, or reordered.

Step 2: Strict Data Hygiene Rules

Ensure zero duplicate codes across Item Codes (itemCode), Vendor Codes (vendorCode), and Customer Codes. Mandatory fields cannot be blank. Standard UOM tokens must be strictly used.

Step 3: Automated Dry-Run Validation

Upon uploading sheets into the MH ERP Admin/Import portal, the system runs an automated validation engine checking foreign keys and data types before committing records into MongoDB.
